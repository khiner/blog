A material defines how a surface responds to light. Select a mesh to assign a material to a part. Select the material to edit its appearance everywhere it is used.

## Surface properties

- [Base surface](/MeshEditor/manual/rendering/materials/surface/) — base color, metallic, roughness, specular reflection, normal maps and emission.
- [Surface layers](/MeshEditor/manual/rendering/materials/layers/) — clearcoat, sheen, anisotropy and iridescence.
- [Transparency and transmission](/MeshEditor/manual/rendering/materials/transparency/) — alpha modes, refraction, volume absorption and diffuse transmission.

## Material assignment

A mesh’s [material slots and variants](/MeshEditor/manual/rendering/material-assignments/) determine which surfaces use each material.
