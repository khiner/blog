Subdivision adds detail to a mesh. Symmetry tools either rebuild one side from the other or align existing vertex pairs.

## Subdivision

| Tool | Where it acts | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subdivide** | Selected edges and faces. | Adds cuts for later edits or displacement and preserves the overall shape. |
| **Subdivision Surface** | Whole mesh. | Rounds the surface with Catmull–Clark subdivision. **Levels** sets the number of subdivisions. |
| **Smooth Surface** | Closed mesh. | Refines curved areas using shading normals. **Cuts** sets the new vertices per edge. Flat shading and sharp marks preserve creases. |

Subdivision changes the silhouette. [Smooth shading](/MeshEditor/manual/modeling/normals/) changes how light varies across the existing surface.

## Symmetrize

**Symmetrize** cuts at a plane, reflects the retained half and welds the seam.

In Edit mode, **Mesh → Symmetrize** adds the operation to the geometry procedure. **Point** places the plane and **Normal** sets its orientation. Reversing the normal exchanges the retained and reflected halves. Edits to the retained side upstream of the node appear on both sides.

## Snap to Symmetry

On a mesh with both halves already present, **Snap to Symmetry** aligns near-matching vertices across the selected axis. Choose which side supplies the reference.

- **Threshold** sets the greatest distance between a reflected vertex and its match.
- **Factor** chooses where each pair aligns. At 0 the reference side stays in place, at 1 the opposite side stays in place, and at 0.5 both move equally.
- **Center** snaps nearby vertices onto the symmetry plane.
