### Truncate

Drop the fractional part, moving toward zero.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="truncate"
  inputs={{
    "In": "Value to truncate.",
  }}
  result="The whole-number part of each component, rounded toward zero."
  signature="unaryFloat"
>

Inputs of 1.8 and −1.8 give 1 and −1. **Floor** gives −2 for the negative input.

</OperationPorts>

### Truncated Modulo

Find the remainder using division rounded toward zero.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="truncatedmodulo"
  inputs={{
    "In1": "The dividend.",
    "In2": "The divisor that sets the repeat interval.",
  }}
  result="A nonzero remainder has the sign of the dividend. A zero divisor gives zero."
  signature="binaryFloat"

With **In1** set to −5 and **In2** to 3, the truncated quotient is −1 and the remainder is −2.

</OperationPorts>

### Wrap

Repeat a value over an interval.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="wrap"
  inputs={{
    "In": "The value to repeat.",
    "Low": "The start of the repeating interval.",
    "High": "The end of the repeating interval.",
  }}
  result="A value between the endpoints, including Low and excluding High. This also works with reversed endpoints. Equal endpoints give their common value."
  fixedTypes={{
    "In, Low, High": "float",
    "Output": "float",
  }}
>

With **Low** at 0 and **High** at 1, an input of 1.25 wraps to 0.25.

</OperationPorts>
