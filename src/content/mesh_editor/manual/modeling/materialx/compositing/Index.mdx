Use **Over** for a premultiplied foreground. For straight-alpha color, use **Matte** or apply **Premultiply** before **Over**.

## Compositing

### Burn

Darken the background with the foreground using color burn.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="burn"
  inputs={{
    "Fg": "Foreground layer or value.",
    "Bg": "Background layer or value.",
    "Mix": "Blend strength between the background and the full effect.",
  }}
  result="Each channel darkens according to the foreground. A foreground channel at or very near zero gives zero at any mix."
  signature="blend"
/>

### Difference

Measure how much two layers differ in each channel.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="difference"
  inputs={{
    "Fg": "Foreground layer or value.",
    "Bg": "Background layer or value.",
    "Mix": "Blend strength. 0 keeps the background. 1 applies the full effect.",
  }}
  result="The absolute channel difference at full mix. Identical inputs give zero."
  signature="blend"
/>

### Disjoint Over

Composite two layers whose coverage occupies separate parts of a pixel.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="disjointover"
  inputs={{
    "Fg": "Foreground layer or value.",
    "Bg": "Background layer or value.",
    "Mix": "Blend strength. 0 keeps the background. 1 applies the full effect.",
  }}
  result="The combined color and coverage of the foreground and background."
  signature="alphaComposite"
/>

### Dodge

Brighten a layer with color dodge.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="dodge"
  inputs={{
    "Fg": "Foreground layer or value.",
    "Bg": "Background layer or value.",
    "Mix": "Blend strength between the background and the full effect.",
  }}
  result="Each channel brightens as the foreground approaches 1. A foreground channel at or very near 1 gives zero at any mix."

### In

Isolate the foreground inside the background’s alpha coverage.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="in"
  inputs={{
    "Fg": "Foreground layer or value.",
    "Bg": "Background layer or value.",
    "Mix": "Blend strength. 0 keeps the background. 1 applies the full effect.",
  }}
  result="Foreground multiplied by background alpha at full mix."
  signature="alphaComposite"
/>

### Inside

Mask a color or value.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="inside"
  inputs={{
    "In": "The value or color to mask.",
    "Mask": "The coverage, from 0 outside to 1 inside.",
  }}
  result="Each channel multiplied by the mask. A mask of 1 preserves the input."
  fixedTypes={{
    "Mask": "float",
  }}
  columns={["In", "Output"]}
  rows={[
    ["float, color3, color4", {"sameAs": "In"}],
  ]}
/>

### Mask

Keep the background in proportion to foreground alpha.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="mask"
  inputs={{
    "Fg": "Foreground layer or value.",
    "Bg": "Background layer or value.",
    "Mix": "Blend strength. 0 keeps the background. 1 applies the full effect.",
  }}
  result="The background multiplied by the foreground’s alpha."
  signature="alphaComposite"
/>

### Matte

Layer a straight-alpha foreground over a background.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="matte"
  inputs={{
    "Fg": "Foreground layer or value.",
    "Bg": "Background layer or value.",
    "Mix": "Blend strength. 0 keeps the background. 1 applies the full effect.",
  }}
  result="The foreground over the background, blended using foreground alpha."
  signature="alphaComposite"
/>

<OperationPorts
  identifier="minus"

    "Fg": "Foreground layer or value.",
    "Bg": "Background layer or value.",
    "Mix": "Blend strength. 0 keeps the background. 1 applies the full effect.",

  signature="blend"
/>

### Mix

Crossfade between two values.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="mix"
  inputs={{
    "Fg": "Foreground layer or value.",
    "Bg": "Background layer or value.",
    "Mix": "The fraction of the foreground to use.",
  }}
  result="Background at 0, foreground at 1 and a linear blend in between."
  columns={["Fg, Bg", "Mix", "Output"]}
  rows={[
    ["float, vector2, vector3, vector4, color3, color4", {"sameAs": "Fg"}, {"sameAs": "Fg"}],
    ["vector2, vector3, vector4, color3, color4", "float", {"sameAs": "Fg"}],
  ]}
>

Set **Mix** to 0.5 to give the foreground and background equal weight.

</OperationPorts>

### Screen

Lighten a background with the foreground.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="screen"
  inputs={{
    "Fg": "Foreground layer or value.",
    "Bg": "Background layer or value.",
    "Mix": "Blend strength. 0 keeps the background. 1 applies the full effect.",
  }}
  result="The complement of the product of the two inverted layers. A black foreground leaves the background unchanged."

/>
