Apply a color conversion when a texture’s RGB encoding differs from the working space. Roughness and normal maps carry numeric data and need no color conversion.

## Adjustment

### Color Correct

Adjust a color’s hue, tonal range and exposure.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="colorcorrect"
  inputs={{
    "Input Color": "The color to adjust.",
    "Hue": "Shifts hues around the color wheel.",
    "Saturation": "Controls color intensity.",
    "Gamma": "Shapes the midtones.",
    "Lift": "Offsets the dark end of the color range.",
    "Gain": "Scales the color channels.",
    "Contrast": "Expands or compresses differences around the contrast pivot.",
    "Contrast Pivot": "The level around which contrast changes.",
    "Exposure": "Adjusts brightness in stops.",
  }}
  result="The corrected color. An alpha channel passes through unchanged."
  fixedTypes={{
    "Hue, Saturation, Gamma, Lift, Gain, Contrast, Contrast Pivot, Exposure": "float",
  }}
  columns={["Input Color", "Output"]}
  rows={[
    ["color3, color4", {"sameAs": "Input Color"}],
  ]}
/>

### Contrast

Expand or compress differences around a pivot.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="contrast"
  inputs={{
    "In": "The value or color to adjust.",
    "Amount": "The contrast multiplier. Values above 1 increase contrast.",
    "Pivot": "The level that stays fixed as contrast changes.",

  result="The input moved toward or away from the pivot."
  columns={["In", "Amount, Pivot", "Output"]}
  rows={[
    ["float, vector2, vector3, vector4, color3, color4", {"sameAs": "In"}, {"sameAs": "In"}],

  ]}
/>

### Range

Map values between two ranges. **Gamma** shapes the transition and **Doclamp** limits the result.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="range"
  inputs={{
    "In": "The value to map.",
    "Inlow": "The source interval’s lower endpoint.",
    "Inhigh": "The source interval’s upper endpoint.",
    "Gamma": "Bends the transition between endpoints. With an ascending output range, values above 1 lift the midtones and values below 1 darken them.",
    "Outlow": "The result at the lower endpoint.",
    "Outhigh": "The result at the upper endpoint.",
    "Doclamp": "Enable to keep the result within the output interval.",
  }}
  result="The remapped value. With Doclamp off, values can extend beyond the output endpoints."
  fixedTypes={{
    "Doclamp": "boolean",
  }}
  columns={["In", "Inlow, Inhigh, Gamma, Outlow, Outhigh", "Output"]}

  ]}
/>

### Remap

Linearly map the input interval onto the output interval.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="remap"
  inputs={{

    "Inhigh": "The source interval’s upper endpoint.",
    "Outlow": "The result at the lower endpoint.",
    "Outhigh": "The result at the upper endpoint.",
  }}
  result="The linearly remapped value. Inputs outside the source interval extend beyond the output endpoints."
  columns={["In", "Inlow, Inhigh, Outlow, Outhigh", "Output"]}

  ]}

Remapping 0–1 to 0.01–0.05 maps 0.5 to 0.03.

## Color transforms

Choose **Add → Colortransform** and select the input color space. Each operation converts the RGB channels to linear Rec. 709 and preserves alpha on four-channel colors.

### ACEScg to Linear Rec. 709

Change from ACEScg’s AP1 primaries to Rec. 709.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="acescg_to_lin_rec709"
  inputs={{
    "In": "Linear ACEScg color.",
  }}
  result="Linear Rec. 709 RGB."
  signature="unaryColor"
/>
