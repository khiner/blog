## Conditional

### And

<OperationPorts
  identifier="and"
  inputs={{

  }}
  result="True only when both conditions are true."
  signature="binaryBoolean"
/>

### Compare

Compare two numbers within a tolerance.

<OperationPorts
  identifier="compare"
  inputs={{
    "In1": "The value to test.",

  result="1 when the absolute difference is within the tolerance, or 0 otherwise."

### Switch

<OperationPorts
  identifier="switch"
  inputs={{
    "In1–In10": "Candidate values in selector order.",

  }}
  result="The selected value. A negative selector gives In1. A selector of 10 or higher gives zero in every component."
