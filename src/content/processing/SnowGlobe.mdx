This uses the Canny edge detection method.
Canny is more complex than some other methods like Sobel, but has better results, especially on noisy images, and isn't much more expensive.

- Click on the image or hit SPACE to start the effect.
- Hold down the mouse to drop snow.
- UP and DOWN keys make it snow more or less.
- SPACE toggles between the original image, the "snowy" image and the edge detection results.
